# Sanfy Disposal: product

- **Platform:** web
- **Stack:** static single-file HTML/CSS/JS (`index.html`), hosted on GitHub Pages
- **Live:** https://10elizabethbell.github.io/sanfyDisposal/

## Users
- **Primary:** homeowners in Sussex and Morris County, NJ who need a dumpster or have junk to clear, often getting a home ready for the holidays. They find the link on Facebook, on a phone, inside Facebook's in-app browser.
- **Secondary:** the owner of Sanfy Disposal, who will see this site first and decide whether it represents the business.

## Purpose
Give a business that lives entirely in one Facebook group ad a real home on the web, and turn a visitor into a text or call to book a dumpster rental or a junk removal.

## Positioning
"Locally Family-Owned, Fast & Reliable!" (their own claim). A local family business, not a franchise; booked by call or text.

## Capabilities
- Two services, in their words: dumpster rental ("Clear out clutter quickly with a dumpster rental") and junk removal ("fast, reliable junk removal").
- Booking by call or text to 973-903-2327 ("Call or text", their wording). The form composes a pre-written text.

## Constraints
- No photos, logo, reviews, prices, hours, owner name, address or town list exist anywhere (Muse, 2026-10-22). Nothing of these may be invented.
- No email published: none on the page.
- Facebook presence is a single post in the "Morris County Business Network" group, not a page.
- Phone-first: 360–430px wide is the design target.

## Brand commitments
- Wordmark only (no logo exists); the palette is chosen, not taken from a brand (inferred: navy + orange, a common disposal pairing, as the brief suggests).
- Voice: "we" (family-owned), upbeat and seasonal, like their ad: "The holidays are coming! Get your home holiday-ready fast with Sanfy Disposal."
- Never reproduce the misspelled hashtag "Homeimpovement".

## Evidence on hand
- One text-only Facebook group ad, 2026-10-22: services sentence, tagline, phone, "Call or text", holiday hook, hashtags #Sussexcounty #Morriscounty.

## Principles
1. Only their own facts; every inference is listed in HANDOFF.md.
2. The first phone screen says who, what, where and how to book.
3. Texting is as easy as calling.
